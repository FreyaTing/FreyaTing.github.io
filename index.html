Understanding the brain.
Tuning emotion.

Yue Ding is an Assistant Research Fellow and master’s supervisor at the Neuroimaging Platform of Shanghai Mental Health Center, Shanghai Jiao Tong University School of Medicine. Yue received a Ph.D. in Biomedical Engineering from Tsinghua University and was a visiting scholar at Johns Hopkins University. Her research investigates the neural mechanisms of non-invasive physical interventions and their applications in mental disorders, bringing together brain–computer interfaces, neuromodulation, affective computing, and music-based interventions.
